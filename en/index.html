A Heritage of Love and Labor

The Lagotto Romagnolo is more than just a pet; it is a centuries-old legacy of Italian craftsmanship. From the marshlands of Comacchio to the forests of Romagna, these intelligent souls have been loyal partners in the hunt for the world's finest truffles.
